# meal-planning

Week planner for a family of three (two adults and a 1-year-old) who shop at Costco, Trader Joe's, or both.

- Choose Costco, Trader Joe's or both stores. The shopping list uses each store's pack sizes and, in "both" mode, sends each item to the store that wastes less.
- Pick healthy breakfasts, lunches and dinners, including bowls, salads, fish and plant-based meals.
- See how many packs the week needs, plus shelf life and freezer guidance.
- Start from a sample week, or let the generator mix proteins, include fish, keep fresh-food waste low, pair weekday breakfasts with that day's lunch, and repeat a recipe when cooking once for two days makes sense.
- Family and settings: adults, little ones and how much of an adult portion they eat, shopping day, whether recipes can repeat in a week, and which days you make lunch alongside breakfast.
- Share a week: the day-by-day plan goes out as text with a link, and opening the link loads that week (after asking) on the other phone. A pasted link works too.
- Share or copy the shopping list as plain text. Ticked items move to an "already in the cart" line.

Live page: https://rjdunlap.github.io/meal-planning/

One static file (`index.html`), no build step. Your plan and ticks are saved in the browser, per device.

Pack sizes and storage times are approximate, and Trader Joe's stock rotates. Check the label. Not affiliated with Costco or Trader Joe's.
